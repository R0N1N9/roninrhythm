01 / About

About me

I'm a social dancer and DJ, and I live on the road. I've danced at more than 200 venues across the US and abroad.

My main styles are Bachata, Kizomba and Fusion. Bachazomba and Kizchata are my names for the blend of Bachata and Kizomba that I'm developing. I also dance some Salsa and Zouk.

Since I'm always traveling, I teach wherever I am: private and couples lessons, workshops for local scenes, and a class before the social when I'm DJing the night.

RoninRhythm is part of RoninSec.

How I teach

  1. Connection first. Frame and embrace before patterns.
  2. Musicality. Dancing to what's actually in the track.
  3. Both roles. Lead and follow technique for everyone.
  4. Floorcraft and consent. How to share a social floor well.
02 / Styles

What I dance

Three distinct partner dances are my home: Bachata, Kizomba and Fusion. I also have working experience in Salsa and Zouk.

Core

Bachata

The Dominican partner dance on the 8-count with a tap on 4 and 8. Fundamentals, sensual styling, and the musicality of the guitar.

Count 1-2-3-tap
Core

Kizomba

The Angolan-rooted connection dance: walking, saidas, pauses, and leading or following with almost no visible movement.

Feel slow · grounded
Core

Fusion

Urban Kiz and fusion partner work: open and closed holds, shapes and isolations, and dancing to whatever the music asks for.

Hold open ↔ close
My blend · in the making

Bachazomba & Kizchata

This is the thing I'm building. It's my own blend of Bachata and Kizomba. Bachazomba carries Kizomba's embrace, weight transfer and walking flow onto Bachata music. Kizchata brings Bachata's footwork, accents and tap inside Kizomba's slow pulse. Come try it in a class, or bring it to your scene as a workshop.

04 / DJ

Behind the decks

DJ sets and music production for partner-dance floors.

After 200+ floors, I know what a room needs and when it needs it. I DJ sets built for partner dancers: tracks you can hear the count in, transitions that don't strand anyone mid-pattern, and an energy curve that keeps people dancing past midnight.

I play Bachata (traditional, modern and sensual), Kizomba, Urban Kiz, Tarraxinha, and fusion crossovers, plus Salsa and Zouk tandas for mixed rooms.
